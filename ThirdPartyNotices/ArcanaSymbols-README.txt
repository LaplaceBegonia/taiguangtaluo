Arcana Symbols Regular is an OFL-licensed subset/combination prepared for the
Android star dice. It embeds 34 required glyphs instead of depending on Windows
system fonts. The font family was renamed to Arcana Symbols.

Sources:
- Noto Sans Symbols Regular, notofonts/noto-fonts, SIL Open Font License 1.1.
  https://github.com/notofonts/noto-fonts/tree/main/hinted/ttf/NotoSansSymbols
  License: NotoSymbols-OFL.txt.
- U+2609 SUN from the project's LXGW WenKai font, SIL Open Font License 1.1.
  License: WenKai-OFL.txt.

Changes: subset to planetary/zodiac symbols and digits; combine U+2609 from
WenKai; remove layout tables unused by individual glyphs; rename family.
